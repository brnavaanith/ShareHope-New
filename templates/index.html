How ShareHope works

A short ledger entry,
not a long process.

Three steps, each with a visible record. You always know whether your offer is pending, accepted, or handed over.

01

Offer, or ask for, exactly what matters

Donors describe quantity, condition and pickup. NGOs publish requirements with needed counts and deadlines.

2 min · guided form
02

Match on category, place and status

Search across eight categories, filter by location and status. NGOs accept offers; donors respond to requirements.

Transparent queue
03

Hand over and close the record

Confirm pickup or drop-off, mark handed over. Both sides keep a receipt line in the donation trail.
